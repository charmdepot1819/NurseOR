Welcome

Know every instrument on the Mayo stand before you scrub in.

NurseOR is a study app for perioperative nurses and nursing students. It teaches the common surgical instruments: what each one looks like, what it is used for, how to tell it from look-alikes, and the nursing practice that goes with it.

1

Instruments

Browse every instrument by category, with its picture, its use, what to look for, and a nursing point. Search by name, use or feature.

2

Flashcards

Look at the picture and name the instrument, then tap to flip. Swipe right if you know it and left if you are still learning. Cards you miss come back sooner.

3

Quiz

Picture ID: see the drawing and choose the correct name. Or take a mixed quiz on uses, features, sharps safety, counts and instrument care. Every answer is explained.

Tips

  • Use the category chips to study one group at a time, for example Clamps.
  • Choose US or UK instrument names. TeachMeSurgery uses UK names such as Spencer Wells and Langenbeck.
  • Look at the tip close-up. Serrations and teeth are how you tell a Kelly from a Crile, or an Allis from a Babcock.
  • On a keyboard: Space flips a card, 1 and 2 mark it, and 1 to 4 answer quiz questions.
  • Your flashcard progress and best quiz score are saved in this browser only.
